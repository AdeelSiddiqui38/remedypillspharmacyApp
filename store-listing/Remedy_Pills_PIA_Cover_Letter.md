Re: Privacy Impact Assessment — Remedy Pills Pharmacy Patient Portal (including a planned Kroll medication-history import feature)

Please find enclosed a Privacy Impact Assessment for the Remedy Pills Pharmacy patient portal application ("the App"), submitted under section 64 of the *Health Information Act*. This is the first PIA submitted for this system, and it is being submitted before the App goes into live operation, so that your office's review can be completed in advance of any real patient information being handled.

To be clear about the App's current status:

- No patient information has been captured or entered into the App or patient portal. The system holds no patient health information at this time.
- Kroll has not been connected to the App, and no information has been pulled from Kroll. The medication-history import feature described in this PIA is designed and built, but it has not been used against any real Kroll data.
- The App is currently listed for testing purposes only (internal/closed testing) and contains no patient data.

This PIA describes the system as designed and built, so that it can be reviewed before launch rather than after. We will not place the App into live use or connect it to Kroll until this review is complete.

This submission includes the Privacy Impact Assessment and its Appendix E-1, the Remedy Pills Pharmacy Privacy & Information Security Policy Manual. We welcome any questions from your office and will respond promptly.
